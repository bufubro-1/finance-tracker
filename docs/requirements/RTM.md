## Gaps and evidence limitations

- Every FR retains a planned story/use-case/component/test/task mapping; milestone mappings describe scoped coverage only. NFRs are cross-cutting constraints, not duplicate user stories.
- All 32 FRs have at least one planned test; all 12 NFRs have verification cases. Mapping does not prove test adequacy or test execution.
- All application components, schemas, adapters, migrations, and executable tests are absent. Every row has an implementation/evidence gap until development produces reviewed code and actual test results.
- DEC-015 approves the milestone/baseline reallocation; remaining owner approvals, host/version support, replay mechanism and proposed numeric/recovery policies remain open in [DECISION_LOG](../DECISION_LOG.md). No approval is inferred from a mapping.
- Business-rule examples are exercised by financial cases, particularly TC-006 through TC-019 and TC-033, TC-035, TC-047, TC-050. BR-014 and BR-015 are covered by TC-025 through TC-028, TC-045, TC-048, and TC-050.
- Runtime optionality is not a traceability gap: Gemini/Telegram are delivered in later phases and may stay disabled.

When requirements change, update the requirement, affected stories/use cases, component responsibilities/model, task dependencies, and planned cases together. Add execution evidence separately; never replace planned status with verified merely because a document exists.
