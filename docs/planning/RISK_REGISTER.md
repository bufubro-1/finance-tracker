# Finance Tracker — Risk Register

Status: Preliminary risks, all **Open**. The solo project owner/developer owns review and response unless explicitly delegated later. These are potential events, not observed defects. Review at each milestone, including the approved M-001A gate at 32 hours, before using real financial data, and after a security, calculation, recovery, or provider incident.

## Qualitative assessment method

Probability is **Low (1)** when exceptional conditions are needed, **Medium (2)** when plausible in ordinary use, and **High (3)** when expected to recur without mitigation. Impact is **Low (1)** for minor inconvenience with no record loss/disclosure, **Medium (2)** for recoverable interruption or misleading output, and **High (3)** for financial-record loss, material calculation errors, or unauthorized disclosure.

Use probability × impact as a prioritization score: **Low = 1–2**, **Medium = 3–4**, **High = 6–9**. Scores are qualitative judgments, not measured probabilities or expected monetary losses. The ratings below are inherent; do not claim residual risk has fallen until controls are implemented and checked.

| Probability \ Impact | Low (1) | Medium (2) | High (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low (1) | Low — 1 | Low — 2 | Medium — 3 |
| Medium (2) | Low — 2 | Medium — 4 | High — 6 |
| High (3) | Medium — 3 | High — 6 | High — 9 |

High risks require mitigation evidence before the affected release feature is accepted. A data-loss, integrity, or disclosure event stops affected writes/sends until diagnosis; it does not require an unrelated cloud or multi-user security platform.

## Monitoring and escalation

Monitor failed integrity checks, rejected allocation conflicts, stale/unpriced holding counts, last successful backup, last scheduler run, failed/uncertain sends, API error classes, and actual effort versus estimates. Use sanitized metadata, not financial payload logging. Each relevant task's acceptance evidence should reference the risk controls it demonstrates.

A proposal awaiting owner review remains a decision, not an implemented mitigation. After implementation, record review date, evidence, residual rating, and whether the owner accepts remaining exposure. Do not close these risks because the register was written.
